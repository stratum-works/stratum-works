How It Works

🎯

Two Controls

Aim and power. That's the whole interface, and it's the whole game.

⚙️

Physics on the Server

Your phone sends two numbers and draws the trajectory that comes back. The simulation never ships inside the app.

🔒

Which Means Scores Are Real

A game whose physics ship in the binary can be solved offline overnight and beaten with one perfect input. Keeping the simulation server-side means there is nothing to extract.

📅

A Daily Attempt

One shot a day. Everyone plays the same hole, and everyone gets the same chance at it.

🏆

Leaderboards Worth Reading

Every score came from the same simulation and can be replayed to confirm it.

🚫

No Tracking

No analytics companies, no data brokers, and no tracking prompt — because there is no tracking.
