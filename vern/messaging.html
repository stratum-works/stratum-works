SMS Messaging Policy

Effective Date: March 18, 2026  |  Last Updated: June 13, 2026

This page describes the SMS and messaging practices for Vern, a smart family assistant service by Stratum Works.

Program Description

Vern sends SMS communications to authorized family members and registered service providers for the purpose of:

  • Family schedule coordination and calendar reminders
  • Weather alerts and daily briefings
  • Household management (chores, shopping lists, meal planning)
  • Service provider appointment scheduling
  • Responding to user-initiated requests and questions

Consent

You are enrolled in Vern messaging when a family administrator adds your phone number to the system or when you create an account through the Vern Companion app. By providing your phone number, you consent to receive automated text messages from Vern. Consent is not a condition of purchase of any goods or services.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your interactions and settings. Typical usage ranges from 1–10 messages per day. You may receive more messages if you actively engage in conversation with Vern.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Vern does not charge for its messaging service, but your mobile carrier's standard messaging rates may apply. Check with your carrier for details.

Opt-Out & Help

Standard Keywords:

STOP — Opt out of all messages

HELP — Get assistance and contact info

START — Re-subscribe to messages

After texting STOP, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further messages will be sent unless you text START to re-subscribe.

After texting HELP, you will receive a message with contact information and a link to this policy.

Supported Carriers

Vern messaging is compatible with all major US carriers including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint, and most MVNOs.

Data Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with third parties is limited to service delivery (e.g., Twilio for SMS delivery) as described in our Privacy Policy.
