Private Family Project

Meet Vern.

Vern is a private family butler for Aaron Wood's family. It handles scheduling, meals, and household coordination from the family's own Mac mini home server, through the Vern Companion iOS app and SMS. Data is stored on that hardware. Cloud AI is optional and opt-in. Vern is not a public product.

What Vern Does

📅

Calendar & Scheduling

Manages family calendars, coordinates schedules across members, and sends reminders before things slip.

🍽️

Meal Planning & Recipes

Suggests meals based on what's in your pantry, tracks nutrition, manages shopping lists, and learns family favorites.

🌤️

Weather & Briefings

Delivers personalized daily digests — weather, calendar, chores, and family updates tailored to each member.

✅

Chores & Tasks

Assigns and tracks household chores with built-in allowance tracking for kids.

💰

Family Finances

A CFO dashboard for your household — budgets, expenses, and financial health at a glance.

💬

Natural Conversation

Talk to Vern like a family member. Through the iOS app, SMS, or both — no interfaces to learn.

Connected Accounts

Vern can optionally connect Google, Microsoft, Yahoo, and Apple or iCloud accounts from the Vern Companion app. It uses them for the household calendar, deconflicting, reminders, and email triage. Triage only labels and archives mail, and never deletes it. The privacy policy covers each provider.

📬

Google

OAuth on the phone requests Gmail (full mailbox, a restricted scope) and Google Calendar. Vern reads messages and events to merge the household calendar, deconflict schedules, send reminders, and triage email. Triage adds labels and archives. It never deletes. Tokens are stored encrypted in a vault on the home server. Google details.

📧

Microsoft

Outlook.com and Microsoft 365 mail and calendar, through the Microsoft identity platform (OAuth) and Microsoft Graph. Same calendar, reminder, and triage uses: labels and archive only, never delete. Tokens are stored encrypted in the home server vault. Microsoft details.

📮

Yahoo

Yahoo Mail and calendar via Yahoo OAuth. Same household calendar, reminder, and triage uses, with the same label-and-archive rule that never deletes mail. Tokens are stored encrypted in the home server vault. Yahoo details.

🍎

Apple and iCloud

iCloud Mail and Calendar. iCloud may use an app-specific password, or the phone may read calendars through EventKit. Credentials are stored encrypted in the home server vault. Email triage follows the same label-and-archive, never-delete rule. Apple details.

Synced mail and calendar data stays stored on the family's Mac mini. Optional remote access is a Cloudflare Tunnel at vern.stratum-works.com. The tunnel reaches that server and does not move the store into the cloud. Cloud AI is off by default. If an admin turns it on and supplies their own API key, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, or xAI Grok may receive mailbox or calendar content for a feature such as email triage, after filtering that redacts key-like and sensitive data. Otherwise Vern uses a local model.
