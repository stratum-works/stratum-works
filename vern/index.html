Private & Family-First

Meet Vern.

A private, AI-powered family assistant that eliminates the mundane so you can focus on the meaningful. Scheduling, meal planning, household coordination, and more — running entirely on your own hardware.

What Vern Does

📅

Calendar & Scheduling

Manages family calendars, coordinates schedules across members, and sends reminders before things slip.

🍽️

Meal Planning & Recipes

Suggests meals based on what's in your pantry, tracks nutrition, manages shopping lists, and learns family favorites.

🌤️

Weather & Briefings

Delivers personalized daily digests — weather, calendar, chores, and family updates tailored to each member.

✅

Chores & Tasks

Assigns and tracks household chores with built-in allowance tracking for kids.

💰

Family Finances

A CFO dashboard for your household — budgets, expenses, and financial health at a glance.

💬

Natural Conversation

Talk to Vern like a family member. Through the iOS app, SMS, or both — no interfaces to learn.

Built on Three Principles

🔒

Privacy-First

All data stays on your hardware. Nothing is sent to the cloud. You own everything.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family-Centered

Built for real families. Age-appropriate content, parental controls, and shared coordination.

🧠

Locally Intelligent

Runs AI on your home server. No subscriptions, no external dependencies, no data harvesting.
