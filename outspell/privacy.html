🔤 Outspell Privacy Terms

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 14, 2026  |  Last Updated: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Outspell, a game by Stratum Works ("we," "us," or "our"), handles information. Outspell is a word game for iPhone and iPad.

The short version: You can play the entire game — including online against other people — without an account and without giving us anything. If you choose to sign in, we store an anonymous identifier from Apple, a display name you pick, and a count of matches you played and finished. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics companies, and no data brokers.

1. Playing without an account

Outspell does not require an account. Single-player games, the campaign, and online matches all work as a guest.

When you play online as a guest, our server holds the match while it is being played, along with a display name you type in for that session. Nothing identifies you, nothing is linked to you, and nothing follows you to another device or another game.

2. If you sign in

Signing in is optional and is offered after you have played, never before. Sign in with Apple is the only sign-in method. There is no password and no email address to remember, and nothing for anyone to leak.

When you sign in, we store:

  • An anonymous identifier from Apple. Apple gives each app a unique, opaque ID for your Apple Account. It is meaningless outside Outspell and cannot be used to identify you anywhere else.
  • A display name you choose. This is the only thing other players see. You may use a generated nickname, type your own, or use your first name.
  • A count of matches you joined and finished. Used to show your completion rate.
  • A session token, so you stay signed in. We store only a cryptographic hash of it, never the token itself.

What we deliberately do not store

  • Your email address. Apple offers it; we do not ask for it and do not keep it.
  • Your surname. Apple offers your full name on first sign-in. We read only your first name, and only to suggest it as a display name — because signing in to a word game should not put your legal name in front of strangers.
  • Your contacts, photos, location, health data, or microphone. Outspell never asks for any of these.

3. Match records

An online match is stored as the random seed it started from and the ordered list of moves played, with the time each move was made. This is how a match survives a server restart and how a game can be replayed to verify it was played fairly.

Match records are shared between the people who played them. If you delete your account, the matches remain — other people played those games too — but nothing in them points at you any more.

4. What we do not do

Outspell contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, no tracking SDKs, and no social media integrations. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone. We do not build a profile of you. We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), and Outspell will never show you the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is no tracking to ask about.

We do keep aggregate statistics about how the game is played — how often a card is used, how long a typical turn takes, how often a challenge succeeds — to balance the game. These are computed from match records described above rather than collected separately, and are used to make the game better, not to study you.

5. Where your data lives

Account data and match records are stored in a PostgreSQL database operated by Stratum Works on Railway, a hosting provider located in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit. Railway processes this data solely to host our service and does not use it for its own purposes.

Apple processes your sign-in. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

These are the only two third parties involved. There are no others.

6. Data retention

  • Account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • Match records are kept indefinitely as part of the game's history.
  • Guest matches are kept only while the match is live and are removed shortly after it ends.
  • Sessions end when you sign out or delete your account.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Account → Delete account inside the app. This is immediate and permanent. Your name, your completion record, and every session are erased, and the seat you occupied in past matches stops pointing at anybody.

Signing out simply removes the session from your device and leaves your account intact.

You may also revoke Outspell's access from your Apple Account at any time: Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple → Outspell.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to certain processing. Deletion is built into the app. For anything else, email us and we will help — there is very little to hand over, which is the point.

We do not sell personal information as that term is defined under United States state privacy laws, and we have no mechanism to do so.

9. Children's privacy

Outspell is a word game suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the game is fully playable without an account, a child can play Outspell without any information being collected at all. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated date. If a change is significant — particularly if we ever begin collecting something new — we will say so in the app rather than only here.
