In Development

Nobody wants to finish the word.

Outspell is built on Ghost, the old spelling game: players take turns adding one letter to a word. Making a word is the whole point — you just don't want to be the one who finishes it.

What Makes It Outspell

📏

A Different Minimum Every Round

Each round sets its own minimum word length. A word that ended the last round is harmless in this one, so the same fragment means something different every time.

🃏

Action Cards

Change a letter, skip your turn, reverse the table, or hand the next player exactly the letter they don't want. Cards are what let anyone at the table compete — not just the best speller in the room.

🎭

Bluffing Is Legal

Play a letter with no word in mind at all. You only lose if somebody calls it and you can't produce one. Challenging on a hunch is just as legitimate.

⏱️

A Strict Clock

About twenty seconds a turn, and it never pauses. Without it a bluff stops being a bluff and becomes something you look up.

🗺️

A Campaign Board

Race around a board for four sites, each won by a duel, then win the centre. Coming second still claims ground.

📡

Live Matches

Open a table, read six characters to a friend, and play. Everyone plays at once — there's no taking your turn tomorrow.

How We Treat You

🚪

No Account Required

Every part of the game works as a guest, online included. Nobody is asked to sign in before they've played.

🕶️

Your Name Is Yours

Play under a made-up name if you like. We only ever read your first name from Apple, never your surname.

🚫

No Ads, No Tracking

No advertising, no analytics companies, no data brokers, and no tracking prompt — because there is no tracking.
